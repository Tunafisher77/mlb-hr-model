# MLB Best Card V1.0

## Output

Exactly three same-game statistical stacks. Each stack contains:

- one projected game winner from the seven published Game Picks;
- one HR candidate from the unchanged HR model;
- two published Player Props from different players in that game.

The HR player cannot also be selected for either Player Prop.

## HR expansion rule

Published HR ranks 1-9 are preferred. HR ranks 10-30 may be used only when the
published HR email does not cover three distinct qualified Game Pick matchups.
Expanded HR candidates are clearly labeled in the Sheet and email.

## Composite score

- Game win probability: 35%
- HR model score: 25%
- Player Prop 1 score: 20%
- Player Prop 2 score: 20%

The three highest complete game stacks are published. No quota is imposed on hitter
versus pitcher props. If three complete stacks do not exist, the model fails instead
of forcing a selection.

## Constraints

- Statistics only
- No sportsbook odds or lines
- No implied probabilities
- No market or gambling information
- Existing Game, HR, and Player Props selection models remain unchanged
